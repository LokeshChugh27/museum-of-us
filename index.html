Exhibit 01 · The origin story

A random night.
A little bit of magic.

On a random night, completely out of nowhere, we met — talked without knowing or even thinking it would ever come this far.

Out of millions and millions of people, somehow I met you. How odd is that? What were the chances? I didn't even know there was a whole group of people behind that night or how any of it would unfold. The conversation just started — so easy, so exciting, so completely different from what I expected to find there that night. We talked and talked. Somehow, you saw through me without really knowing me, without ever meeting me. We stayed there much longer than we intended, and honestly, I didn't want to stop talking to you. The whole night was full of surprises. How we started. How, out of so many people, I ended up meeting you. How I somehow shared more with you than I probably should have. And how, after all of that, we somehow ended up adding each other on Instagram. I still think about how strange and beautiful that is. Out of all those millions of people, I bumped into one of the best people I've ever met — or, at least so far, the best person I've ever met. And for me, that night felt magical.

“Out of millions and millions of people, somehow, I met you.”

Exhibit 02 · Why you

Oh, where do I start?
It wasn't one reason.

There are so many little reasons and moments that made you so special to me — so many that, without me even realizing it, I started missing you, started loving you, and somehow you became a part of me and my favorite person.

But from the very beginning, you stood out from everyone I had ever met. A random conversation on a random night that could have ended after a few minutes and never meant anything. But it didn't. We kept talking. And somewhere between the jokes, the random conversations, and the hours passing without us noticing, you stopped feeling like a stranger. The way talking to you started feeling easy. The way I began looking forward to your messages. The way a conversation could start with something completely random — just pure jibber-jabber — and end with me wishing it wasn't over. I don't think I ever sat down and decided, “This is why I like her.” I think I just kept finding more reasons to choose you without realizing I was doing it. Falling more and more in love with you each and every day, whether we are talking or not. With time, you started to feel like home to me. Coming to you makes me forget everything for a while. You give me this different, weird kind of comfort and peace. You light up my day just by being in it, and you light up my world by being part of it. It's like before meeting you, my world was all black and white — dark and gray. Then you put all the color into it with your cute smile, perfect cheekbones, and those green, color-changing, magical eyes.

Your voice

Somehow, hearing you lights me up, makes me so happy and excited, and leaves me thinking about how I will ever be able to resist you when we're finally together in person — because I already know I'm going to want to stop you and kiss you every time. Hehe.

Your randomness

You can send me something completely unnecessary and still make me want to know the story behind it. Or send me pictures — when you do your tattoo, when you change your hair, or just little moments from your day — and somehow I always want to see more.

A letter from the museum · 09

A letter,
for you.

In my whole life, I have always had this habit of looking for something more or something different. No matter what I had, whether it was where I was living, the things I was doing, the games I was playing, or just life in general, a part of me would always look at something else and wonder what it would be like to have that, to try that, or to experience something different. But with you, somehow that search ended❤️. For once, I didn't look somewhere else and wonder if there was something better. I never thought about wanting someone different or wished you were different in any way. There were no second guesses about you, no doubts, no feeling that maybe I should look for something else. I just wanted you🫶. And that's something I don't think I have ever felt before. You are just so perfect the way you are for me. The way you smile. The way you giggle. The way you make faces🥹, and so many other little things about you. And I want to experience so many things, want so many things with you. But I don't know how far we will be able to. Will it be able to work out? When will we be able to stay in the same place🌎❤️? Beside all that, I wish the very best for you. Wish you all the good luck there is for your future. I want you to be happy and have all the success you desire in life✨. I know people say there is always sorrow that comes with happiness, and nothing can be forever. But I want most of the time for you to be happy and healthy. There can be a little bit of sorrow, sad times, but I'm hoping they won't last long. And I also heard that without sad times or sorrow, we won't be able to appreciate happiness that much. So it's good in a way. So I want all the rollercoaster ride🎢. I don't wish for you to be happy always, but I want the trajectory to be mostly happy times for you. Regardless of how far or how long we go, I don't have more words to tell you or express how much you mean to me or how much I love you❤️. So, wish you a Happy Birthday🎂 and colorful, happening years ahead🌈✨.
